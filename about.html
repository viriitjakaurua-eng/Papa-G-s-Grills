ABOUT PAPA G'S

A LOCAL GRILL WITH A BOLD PERSONALITY.

Our Story Papa G's Chicken, also known as Papa G's Grill, is a proudly Namibian food brand based in Windhoek. Built around a passion for great chicken and bold flavour, Papa G's has become a popular local destination for customers looking for quick, affordable and satisfying meals. From our flame-grilled chicken to our traditional pap and signature Papa G's Chakalaka, we bring together familiar flavours and generous portions that keep our customers coming back. Today, Papa G's serves customers across Windhoek, with outlets in Grysblock, Pionierspark and the CBD. As we continue to grow, our goal remains simple: serve great food, create memorable experiences, and bring people together one meal at a time. Papa G's Chicken — Real Food. Real Flavour..

Our signature combination of chicken, pap and Papa G's Chakalaka brings together comfort, spice and that smoky grill taste.

Whether you're stopping by for lunch, feeding your family or ordering for delivery, we want every Papa G's meal to be memorable.
